## Design Context

### Users

EnggXR serves EAPCET students, usually aged 16 to 20, and the parents helping them make a high-stakes college decision. They often use ordinary Android phones, may have inconsistent connectivity, and need to act during time-sensitive counselling rounds.

### Brand Personality

Precise, reassuring, and forward-looking. The product should feel like a trusted admissions decision instrument. It should reduce confusion without creating false urgency or implying guaranteed admission.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light, calm reading surface for decision-heavy content and a restrained dark technical shell for navigation and operational summaries. EnggXR navy and cyan are the primary brand colors. The interface should be clear, compact, and credible, with no decorative gradients, permanent glow, gaming language, or generic card grids.

### Design Principles

- Lead with the decision the student needs to make.
- Keep admission uncertainty, sources, and demonstration-data warnings visible.
- Preserve all important actions and explanations on phones and tablets.
- Use cyan sparingly for actions, selection, and verified computation.
- Keep touch targets at least 44 by 44 points and support small phones, tablets, safe areas, and keyboards.
